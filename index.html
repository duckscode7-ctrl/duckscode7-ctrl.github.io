DUCKS uploader

A private upload tool for the DUCKS YouTube channels · Contact: duckscode7@gmail.com

What it does

DUCKS uploader is a private command-line tool that runs only on the channel owner's personal computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our own original short-form videos (YouTube Shorts) to our own YouTube channels: 분양덕, 경제 읽어주는 덕희, 얌얌덕 and 눈나. All of these channels belong to the Google account duckscode7@gmail.com.

How it uses Google data

  • youtube.upload — to upload our videos to our channels.
  • youtube.readonly — only to confirm which of our channels the sign-in belongs to, before each upload.

No viewer, subscriber, comment or other third-party data is accessed. Nothing is shared, sold or shown to anyone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the channels above. The tool has no public sign-up and no other users. Each upload runs only after the owner approves that specific video.
